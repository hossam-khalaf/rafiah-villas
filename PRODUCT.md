# Product

## Register
product

## Users
High-net-worth individuals, luxury villa buyers, and real estate investors in Saudi Arabia. They expect premium experiences that respect their time—prioritizing clarity, live inventory, and exact architectural specifications over flowery sales copy.

## Product Purpose
A high-end, availability-first digital showroom. It serves as a rapid, filter-first discovery engine allowing users to explore live villa inventory, study floor plans, and effortlessly register interest.

## Brand Personality
Confident, severe, architectural, and strictly utilitarian.

## Anti-references
- Generic real estate "brochure" templates
- SaaS-style bubbly interfaces and soft gradients
- Meaningless auto-playing video heroes with generic "Discover Luxury" copy
- Confusing scroll-hijacking experiences

## Design Principles
- **Show, Don't Tell:** Data (availability, price, area) leads the experience.
- **Architectural Severity:** Brutal, clean lines with stark typography.
- **Frictionless Discovery:** Snappy interactions, preserved search state, and progressive disclosure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA compliance. Use high contrast combinations and ensure focus states and error states are universally legible.
